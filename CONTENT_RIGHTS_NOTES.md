# DaoYin / 道印 Content Rights Notes

This project uses a conservative content policy for public preview and future production.

## Safe Content Principles

1. Classical Chinese source text may be used when it is verified as public-domain material.
2. Do not copy modern translations, commentary, article text, product copy, or database editorial notes from third-party sites.
3. English interpretations should be original DaoYin editorial copy.
4. Product images must be original photography, licensed assets, or vendor-provided assets with permission.
5. Fonts must be open-source, system fonts, or commercially licensed.
6. Generated images should be reviewed for cultural accuracy and should not imitate a living artist or identifiable brand style.

## Yi Jing / 周易 Handling

The Chinese original of the Yi Jing / Zhouyi is an ancient classical text. The prototype treats brief classical Chinese lines as public-domain source material.

Production recommendation:

- Build a curated internal text corpus from verified public-domain editions.
- Store original Chinese separately from DaoYin's own English explanations.
- Cite the classical source in cultural pages.
- Avoid copying a third-party website's full edited database, punctuation choices, translations, or commentary wholesale.

## Translations And Commentary

Modern translations and commentaries are usually separately copyrightable. Do not copy:

- Published English translations.
- Modern Chinese explanations.
- Website summaries.
- Book commentary.
- Course materials.

Instead:

- Write original explanations.
- Use short classical excerpts only where needed.
- Keep a source log for editorial review.

## Product And Ritual Content

For Kai Guang / 开光 and Recorded Consecration / 实地开光录制:

- Record original videos.
- Confirm permission from any site, host, or practitioner shown.
- Avoid filming third-party copyrighted music, artwork, signage, or private materials.
- Ensure the DaoYin ID / 道印编号 is visible in the recording if this service is selected.

## Current Prototype Notice

The current prototype does not use third-party product photos. Visual product blocks are CSS placeholders. English interpretations are prototype copy and should be reviewed before launch.
